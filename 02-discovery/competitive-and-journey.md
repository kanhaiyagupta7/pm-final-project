# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A streaming viewer who enjoys exploring content but struggles to decide what to watch from a large library.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Find something genuinely interesting to watch without spending too much time browsing.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Priya spends 20 minutes browsing, cannot find anything worth watching, closes the app, and goes back to watching DVDs
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** IMDb / Rotten Tomatoes / Metacritic 
Reddit / YouTube reviews / trailers
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Browses rows and carousels, opening a few title pages and trailers.
Leaves the app to check IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, or a “best on [platform]” listicle.
Keeps a shortlist in her head, in screenshots, or in open tabs, then goes back and forth between options.
Gives up after about 20 minutes, closes the app, and puts on a DVD.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The breaking point is the 20-minute mark: after all her scrolling and checking, she still can’t commit to a title. She closes the app empty-handed and picks a DVD instead.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** In user interviews, Priya described the moment directly: “I spent 20 minutes scrolling, couldn’t find anything, and ended up putting on a DVD I’ve already seen.” Session data shows the same pattern: long browsing sessions with multiple title-page views and no play event before the app is closed.
