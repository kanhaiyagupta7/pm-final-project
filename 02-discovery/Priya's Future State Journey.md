# Priya's Future State Journey

Streaming discovery initiative: four-stage future state for the human-led curation strategy.

## Context

- **Persona:** Priya, a streaming viewer who enjoys exploring content but struggles to decide what to watch from a large library. Her goal is to find something genuinely interesting without spending too much time browsing.
- **Friction today:** She browses for about 20 minutes, finds nothing worth watching, closes the app, and goes back to watching DVDs.
- **Strategy:** Restore brand authority and win back users by prioritizing human-led curation and restoration over mass-market scale.
- **Value proposition:** Less scrolling, more confidence.

## Timeline at a glance

| Stage | User action | Internal state | Pain point addressed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Set the mood | Picks mood and time -> gets a focused start | Curious, a little unsure | Huge library, no starting point |
| 2. See the shortlist | Reads 5 editor picks -> trusts them without leaving | Interested, reassured | Checking IMDb and listicles elsewhere |
| 3. Preview and decide | Watches a short preview, saves one -> commits with ease | Confident, in control | Shortlist scattered across tabs and notes |
| 4. Press play | Hits play -> watches instead of reaching for a DVD | Satisfied, relieved | 20 minutes of browsing, then giving up |

## The four stages

Each bullet follows an Action -> Benefit format.

### 1. Set the mood

- **User action:** Picks a mood and time window -> gets a focused starting point.
- **Internal state:** Feels curious but unsure -> open to guidance, not a blank grid.
- **Pain point addressed:** Replaces endless rows -> removes the overwhelming, directionless start.

### 2. See the shortlist

- **User action:** Reads five human-curated picks, each with a "why" -> trusts them in-app.
- **Internal state:** Feels interested and reassured -> sees real editorial judgment, not an algorithm.
- **Pain point addressed:** Adds editor context in-app -> removes the trip to IMDb and listicles.

### 3. Preview and decide

- **User action:** Watches a short preview and saves one pick -> commits without comparing tabs.
- **Internal state:** Feels confident and in control -> stops second-guessing.
- **Pain point addressed:** Saves the pick in-app -> ends screenshots, notes, and open tabs.

### 4. Press play

- **User action:** Presses play within minutes -> watches instead of reaching for a DVD.
- **Internal state:** Feels satisfied and relieved -> keeps coming back.
- **Pain point addressed:** Turns browsing into viewing -> ends the 20-minute give-up moment.

## Three competitive advantages over the manual workaround

1. **Trust without leaving the app.** Human-curated reasons replace IMDb, listicles, and Reddit, so the decision and the engagement signal stay with the platform.
2. **One shortlist in one place.** Saved picks replace screenshots, tabs, and mental lists, so intent is captured and Priya can return to it.
3. **A faster path to play that improves.** Her choices refine future picks, while a DVD shelf stays finite and learns nothing.

## Assumptions to validate

- Only the 20-minute browsing session and the DVD fallback come from the persona. The outside checks (IMDb, listicles) and the manual shortlist are assumptions to confirm in research.
- Stage 2 assumes each curated pick comes with a short editor's reason, which is what makes human-led curation visible to the user.
- No target times (such as minutes to first play) are set yet. Add them once baseline metrics are available.
