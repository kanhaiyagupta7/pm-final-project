🎯 Product Strategy & Decision Principle

Primary metric: User able to view the film they want to view.
Persona: Priya — a streaming viewer who enjoys exploring content but struggles with too many choices.
Moment of misery: Users cannot find content worth watching.
Guardrail: Keep users within the StreamLine streaming experience.

Product decision: Prioritize discovery and decision confidence over additional features. Validate curated discovery first, then invest in AI-powered relevance and personalization based on observed playback outcomes.
