# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** Priya, a streaming viewer who enjoys exploring content but struggles to decide what to watch from a large library. Her goal is to find something genuinely interesting without spending too much time browsing.
- **Primary metric:** user able to view the film that they want to view
- **Moment of misery:** Users cannot find content worth watching
- **Guardrail:** should not drop and deviate away from the streaming app
